About

Since launching in 2017 The everyday wonders are often hidden in our daily routines — the ever-changing shapes of clouds, a glittering sunset, a wildflower blooming in the cracks of sidewalks. Take a moment to notice these moments of magic that make life sparkle over a cup of specialty coffee envisioned by award-winning pop superstar JJ Lin.

Sip on the most raved about artisan Pour Over Coffee, made with Miracle Coffee roasted, single origin beans. Created exclusively for Singapore, Kaya Cloud is an Americano-style coffee topped with velvety fresh cream cap flavoured with kaya, complete with shaved gula melaka. Other Singapore-exclusive pastries and sandwiches include Rainbow Cloud, Kopi Choux Puff and Rainbow Roti.

#MiracleCoffeeSG #MiracleCoffeeMBS

Miracle Coffee specializes in premium hand-brewed coffee. With only single origin coffee beans used to perfectly present the natural flavours and richness of the beans. The selection of beans is rotated monthly and roasted in-store.Different countries of origin, different roasts, different tones. Bringing you a different flavour profile each time. So that you can start each day anew with something to always look forward to.

Sip your way into joy this holiday season with our newest creation, "The Miracle 4"! Indulge in the perfect blend of flavors that'll make your taste buds dance.

These NEW beans have been sourced from some of the best coffee farms and carefully roasted for their unique flavours and aromas. Whether you are a fan of refreshing fruity Panama or a winey aromatic Columbia, we have got just the one for you.
